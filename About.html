The story

Behind the kit

Sticks up, always

I'm Donny, and drumming is the one thing I refuse to do halfway. Whatever's the deal; a club stage, a tour van, a home studio, a student's first paradiddle... I bring the same relentless drive.

Metal is my specialization: blast beats, breakdowns, and the kind of pocket that makes a room move. But you probably know that good drumming offers a bit more than just some speed and breaking cymbals.

I always aim to add creativity and a vibe that fits the music like a glove. That's also why I always want to learn more new, weird things. If your genre is something besides metal, unusual, or just plain hard to categorize, I want in.

Beyond playing live, I write and program drum parts and full MIDI-packages for bands who need studio-tight drums without booking a studio, and I teach lessons for anyone who wants to hit as hard (and as accurately) as I do.
